## About this lesson

- **about**: `Nature as something to be controlled explores the idea that humans have the ability to change nature, but that this ability comes with responsibility. The decisions we make now, will impact the future.`
- **description** (LessonCard line, from the resource-library card): `This lesson explores the idea that humans can change nature, but that does comes with responsibility.` ("does comes" is a typo in Figma; suggest "that this comes with responsibility".)

### Students learn to (**goals**)

1. `how human choices affect both people and nature`
2. `the difference between short-term and long-term outcomes`
3. `when to act, and when to act with care`

## Lesson context (**context**)

`The lesson is centered around a real, local situation: a rice field where insects are reducing the harvest. Children are invited to think carefully about this situation using a guided website and a reflection worksheet.`

## The lesson in five steps (**steps**)

The heading says "five steps" but there are **four** numbered steps (the site generates the heading from the count).

1. **title** `Introduction`
   **text** `The teacher introduces the situation using the website: rice as essential food, insects reducing the harvest, and rising prices. Children are invited to think carefully rather than choose quickly.`
2. **title** `Exploring two choices`
   **text** `Using the website, the class explores two possible ways to respond:\na fast solution using strong pesticides\na slower, gentler method that works with nature\nThe teacher emphasizes that every choice has benefits and costs.`
   (Lines 2–3 are a list in the design.)
3. **title** `Listening to Many Voices`
   **text** `Children choose which perspectives to listen to and in what order, including people, nature, and the future. Each voice offers a different view on the same decision.`
4. **title** `Reflection and evaluation`
   **text** `Children complete a worksheet reflecting on short- and long-term effects and how the choice feels. Kids may share their insights with the class.`

## Key message / Teacher tip

- **keyMessage**: `This lesson helps children understand that controlling nature requires ethical evaluation and future thinking.`
- **teacherTip**: `Use a calm, neutral tone.\nAllow children to choose the order in which they listen to voices.\nEncourage listening before speaking.\nAvoid guiding children toward a specific solution.\nEmphasize that careful thinking takes time.` (five lines; a list in the design)

## Lesson context (**context**)

## The lesson in five steps (**steps**)

The heading says "five steps" but there are **four** numbered steps. The site builds the heading from the number of steps ("The lesson in {n} steps"), so it will say four.

1. **title** `Introduction`
   **text** `The teacher introduces the problem;\nplastic is used every day; some plastic ends up in nature and the sea; this affects animals, people, and places. Children share observations and perspectives. The focus is on understanding the problem, not solving it yet.`
2. **title** `Introduction to Brainstorming`
   **text** `The teacher explains what brainstorming is:\nsharing many ideas\nno judging\nbuilding on each other’s thoughts\nChildren are told that all ideas are welcome.`
   (Lines 2–4 are a list in the design.)
3. **title** `Group Design Workshop`
   **text** `In small groups, children brainstorm ideas on large paper.\nAfter brainstorming:\ngroups discuss their ideas\nchildren vote with stickers\none idea is selected and developed into a poster\nThe teacher moves between groups to support collaboration.`
   (Lines 3–5 are a list.)
4. **title** `Sharing and Reflection`
   **text** `Groups present their posters to the class.\nPosters may be displayed in the classroom to keep ideas visible over time.`

## Key message / Teacher tip

- **keyMessage**: `This lesson helps children understand that caring for nature starts locally, and that they can become actors in making change possible.`
- **teacherTip**: `Encourage quieter children to speak.\nRemind children that disagreement can be productive.\nAvoid evaluating ideas as good or bad.\nEmphasize that small ideas can still matter.` (four lines; a list in the design)
